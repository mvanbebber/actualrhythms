# Drum samples

From the Salamander Drumkit by Alexander Holm, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderDrumkit (files taken from the trimmed copy at
https://github.com/SoerenSofke/Salamander-Drumkit-Trim). The overhead (OH) recording of one hit per
kit piece, trimmed with a short fade and converted to mono MP3. These files are shared under the
same license.

- kick.mp3: kick_OH_F_1
- snare.mp3: snare_OH_F_1
- tom.mp3: hiTom_OH_F_1 (12" rack tom)
- tom-low.mp3: loTom_OH_MP_1 (14" floor tom; also the medium tom, played a minor third higher)
- hihat.mp3: hihatClosed_OH_F_1
- hihat-open.mp3: hihatOpen_OH_F_1
- hihat-foot.mp3: hihatFoot_OH_MP_1 (pedal)
- ride.mp3: ride1_OH_MP_1 (Paiste 20" medium ride)
- crash.mp3: crash1_OH_FF_1 (Paiste 18" medium crash)

`offsets.json` (tools/instrument_offsets.py) holds each sample's onset, loudness and peak.
