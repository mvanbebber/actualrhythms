# Licence of the rhythm data (`rhythms.js`)

`rhythms.js` is a compilation of short rhythmic excerpts (with their pitches) derived from:

- public-domain scores in the music21 corpus (https://web.mit.edu/music21/);
- Scott Joplin's rags from *A Digital Edition of Scott Joplin's Music*, © 2004–2021 Craig Stuart Sapp,
  https://github.com/craigsapp/joplin, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0;
- lead sheets from OpenEWLD (https://github.com/00sapo/OpenEWLD). Songs first performed after 1930 are not
  public domain in the US; they are included with permission obtained by the owner of Actual Rhythms, and that
  permission does not extend to anyone reusing this data. Rebuild without `--openewld-all` for a version
  containing only US-public-domain songs;
- the Weimar Jazz Database via the jazzodata package (https://github.com/klausfrieler/jazzodata), MIT licence,
  © Klaus Frieler, Martin Pfleiderer, Wolf-Georg Zaddach, Jakob Abeßer.

**Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International** licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), except for the permission-based OpenEWLD songs noted above,
whose rights stay with their owners. You may reuse the rest for non-commercial purposes if you credit
the sources above and share your adaptations under the same licence.
