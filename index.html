Timing calibration

Calibrate plays 16 clicks; tap along with the last 12, just as you would in practice, and the offset is measured for whatever you're listening on (see Listening on). Recalibrate if you switch tap clicks on or off.

Tempo

Tempo counts the beat: quarter notes in simple time, dotted quarters in 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8 (and dotted halves in 6/4).
