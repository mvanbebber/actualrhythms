Genres

Folk tunes are most of the pieces. Jazz and classical give the most rhythms.

A folk tune is short, so it gives a few rhythms. A string quartet movement or a recorded solo runs for hundreds of bars, and nearly every few bars is a new rhythm.

Difficulty

Jazz reads hardest, Bach easiest.

Each genre's rhythms by level, from steady beats (1) to triplets, 32nds and sixteenth syncopation (5). Jazz is the hardest: 77% of its rhythms are level 5, then ragtime at 53%. Bach's chorales are the gentlest, half of them at level 2. Folk tunes sit at level 4, the sixteenths of reels and hornpipes.
